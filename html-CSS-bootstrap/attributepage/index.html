Acharya Prashant

About:

Acharya Prashant (born Prashant Tripathi on March 7, 1978, in Agra, India) is an Indian philosopher, author, Vedanta teacher, and social reformer.

Background and Education:

• Academic Credentials: He is an alumnus of IIT Delhi and IIM
• Civil Services: He is a former civil services officer who left his corporate career in 2006 to focus on education and spiritual teachings.
• Foundation: He founded Advait Life-Education and the PrashantAdvait Foundation to structure his teachings and public outreach.

Life Work:

• Vedanta & Scriptures: He is widely known as a Vedanta exegete who interprets texts such as the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, and the Ashtavakra Gita. His approach focuses on questioning the ego, cutting through blind faith and superstition, and encouraging self-inquiry rather than dogmatic ritual.
• Social & Environmental Causes: Beyond philosophy, he is an active advocate for veganism (recognized by PETA India as an influential vegan voice), environmental protection, and women's liberation.
• Writing and Media: He has authored over 100 books—including national bestsellers like Karma—and maintains a massive digital presence with millions of subscribers across his online video and audio repositories. As noted by users on Reddit, there is a broad consensus that his technical and corporate background shapes his logical, analytical approach to traditional spiritual concepts.
